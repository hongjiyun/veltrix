The Beginning of Veltrix

The Convergence of Data and Possibility

Veltrix is a compound word combining "Velocity," representing the speed of movement, and "Matrix," signifying a complexly intertwined data network.

We are an intelligence platform supporting all enterprises to reach the pinnacle of business (Vertex) by organizing today's complex data networks at overwhelming speed.

Our Goal

Technology for Everyone

Our goal is to build an environment where anyone can intuitively utilize data and cutting-edge technology, regardless of their technical expertise. We aim to eliminate operational complexities and empower every team to unlock the true value of data.

The Future We Imagine

Infinite Expansion into Global Markets

Veltrix transcends regional and national boundaries to continuously evolve into a global business infrastructure. Powered by scalability that accommodates enterprises of all sizes, we will become the standard for data stacks in the global market.

Core Values

1. Accessibility

Making complex data analysis accessible to everyone. We deliver a user-friendly environment where high-level technology can be utilized easily and affordably.

2. Global Scalability

From startups to global enterprises. We pursue scalable technology that adapts flexibly across business sizes and time zones without limits.

3. Innovation & Adaptability

Responding agilely to rapidly changing market environments. We create new values through continuous innovation and flexible adaptation.

4. Trust & Security

Maintaining a world-class, high-standard security architecture. We build a robust foundation where global enterprises can manage their data with absolute peace of mind.
